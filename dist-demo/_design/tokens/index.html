The Lumberjack's Archive

Paper folders in five cardstocks and a flannel lining, with a navy bear who is welcome in every room of the lab.

This page is a temporary design study, not the GusmaoLab homepage. It shows the palette, typefaces, paper materials and section environments. The colours are the Chromatic Archive, approved and locked by the owner (P09-A01); the typefaces were approved in P03.

Bioinformatics, AI, personal projects, literary experiments and occasional mathematical puns all have to feel at home here, so nothing is stern and nothing is shouting.

  • IdentityThe bear-pride stripe family: brown, rust, gold, tan, white, gray, black.
  • CharacterBearnard: midnight-navy fur, green eyes, amber glasses, cream markings.
  • AtmosphereA quiet flannel lining around the archive, never behind text.
  • StructureReal paper folders, each category in its own cardstock.

Specimen A

Identity palette

Twelve foundation colors are the same on every page: the locked shell, link and V colors, the text inks and the identity. Six supporting tones serve specific roles. The cardstock is separate: five category palettes, below. Components never use these primitives directly, only semantic roles. The hex values are read from src/styles/tokens.css andsrc/styles/categories.css when the site is built.

Specimen B

Typography

Four roles, all from fonts already installed on readers' devices, so nothing is downloaded or redistributed. Where a face is missing, its stack falls back to a close relative.

Specimen C

Paper and materials

A small number of surfaces, each a step from the last. Depth comes from edges and soft shadows as much as from lightness, so deeper folders never jump in brightness.

  • Edge highlight--color-folder-edge
  • Recess--shadow-recess
  • Resting paper--shadow-paper
  • Raised paper--shadow-paper-raised
Bearnard

Mascot slot

Reserved for an approved Bearnard derivative. This study shows no mascot image: the canonical masters stay untouched and out of the site.

  • Navy fur
  • Green eyes
  • Amber glasses
  • Flannel

Specimen D

Categories and environments

One folder system. A section's category chooses its colors (data-category: flannel, folder and paper) and its environment chooses its typography and texture (data-environment). The shell, links, accent and V are the same in every panel. These are the pairings the site uses.

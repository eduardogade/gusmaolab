Design study · P05 · Temporary

Physical Folder Geometry — P05 Visual Study

Paper folders with index tabs, nested up to three levels deep. This is a temporary design study, not the GusmaoLab website and not production content. The owner approved the folder appearance.

  • Every tab, label and order comes from the P04 navigation registry, except the third level in cases C, G and H, which is a synthetic fixture.
  • An open folder always has a selected tab in its row of children: its Overview when the folder's own page is open (P06).
  • Tabs here are labels; on the site's pages they are links (P06). Hover an unopened folder that has folders inside it to see its preview (P07); here the previews are pictures too, and their items are not links. The full small-screen strategy is P08.
  • Rows that do not fit scroll sideways inside their folder, never the page. A fade at an edge means more tabs lie beyond it.

Synthetic fixture · sample content

Projects

Three folders and the page, nested: the level 1 and level 2 cardstocks, then the category's lightest material for the third folder and its paper, set apart by edges and shadows. Deeper content, such as a single project, would open here, inside this third folder, never as a fourth row of tabs.

  • Each level adds a small inset, so the reading area stays wide.
def gc_content(sequence: str) -> float:
    """Share of G and C bases in a DNA sequence."""
    return sum(base in "GC" for base in sequence) / len(sequence)

# Design system

Status: summary written in P01 (2026-11-10). Only the direction is approved. Concrete values are **To be supplied**, expected in Wave II (P05–P09).

## Identity

**The Lumberjack's Archive — Simplified.**

- Palette sources: the bear-pride flag and Bearnard's blue/green/Terrain visual identity.
- Mood: a cozy lumberjack aesthetic.
- Sections may use different palettes and levels of ornamentation while keeping one shared identity.
- Humor is welcome throughout, including the CV and Research pages.
- Bearnard v1.3 and approved variants are part of the identity. See `assets/bearnard/README.md`.

## Folder invariants (visual)

- Real physical paper folders with horizontal tabs, never a computer file manager.
- The active tab has a V-shaped indicator.
- Inactive tabs stay clearly visible.

The navigation rules are in `information-architecture.md`.

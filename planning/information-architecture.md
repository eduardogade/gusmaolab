## Navigation: physical folder tabs

1. Navigation uses real physical folder tabs with nested folder bodies.
2. At most three tab levels are visible.
3. Clicking a tab opens its actual destination page.
4. Hovering an expandable tab previews only its immediate children.
5. The active tab has a V-shaped indicator. Inactive tabs remain clearly visible.
6. No persistent sidebar.
7. No breadcrumb interface.
8. No computer-file-manager design.
9. Content deeper than the third level is handled within the body of the third-level folder.

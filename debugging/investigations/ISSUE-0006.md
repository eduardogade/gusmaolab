# ISSUE-0006 — Legacy design studies are routed in the release build

Status: **resolved by D-157 (the owner chose option A: gate all six).** Found by the P22 route audit (QA-ROUTE-07, `development/qa-exceptions.json`).

## What is there

Six design studies from P03 to P12 are generated by every build, `npm run build` included:

`/_design/tokens/`, `/_design/folders/`, `/_design/bearnard/`, `/_design/records/`, `/_design/editorial/` (an overview and eight collections with their synthetic entries) and `/_design/quarto/` (with `public/_design/quarto/` assets). 36 pages in all.

They contain synthetic content (invented editorial entries, a records study from `src/design/records-fixture.ts`) and are labelled as studies. Every later specimen (P13-A01 onward) is routed only when `demosEnabled()` is true (D-105) and the release build is searched for it; these predate that rule.

## Why it matters

If the site were deployed from `dist/` today, these 36 pages would be public. The repository's own rules say synthetic fixtures must not be published as content, and the P22 plan says development-only routes are excluded from ordinary builds.

## Why P22 did not change it

Five older test files assert that the studies are in `dist/`: `tests/smoke-test.mjs`, `tests/routes.mjs`, `tests/folders.mjs`, `tests/bearnard.mjs` and `tests/records.mjs` (and `tests/editorial-layouts.mjs` reads the editorial study). Gating them is a small change per page (`getStaticPaths` returns `[]` unless `demosEnabled()`), but moving those assertions to the demonstration build is a decision about the approved P03 to P12 work, and the owner has not made it. P22 records it as a bounded exception: it is shown on every report as a **release blocker**, and a *new* development page in the release fails the run.

## Options

1. Gate the six studies like the later specimens, and move the old tests' reads to `dist-demo/` (recommended before P24).
2. Remove the studies and their tests.
3. Keep them public on purpose (the owner says so, and the exception's wording changes).

## Resolution (D-157)

Nine route files return no static paths unless `demosEnabled()`; the Quarto study's figure moved out of `public/` to `src/design/quarto/assets/` behind a gated route; `scripts/demo-check.mjs` and the QA route audit fail on any path under `_design/` in a release. A release has 37 pages. The studies' tests read a throwaway demonstration build (`tests/demo-dist.mjs`); five browser suites read `dist-demo/`. The exception in `development/qa-exceptions.json` was removed.
